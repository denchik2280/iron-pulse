IRON PULSE - RECORDED SOUND LIBRARY
Imported 16 October 2026. No background music is included.

All included sound recordings are CC0 1.0 Universal / public domain dedication.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
The following credits are preserved voluntarily, with source pages and exact source-file edits
in SampleEdits.json. Runtime audio is loaded from these recordings, never synthesized.

1. THE FREE FIREARM SOUND LIBRARY
Recorded by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras, and Matthew Nanney.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
Archive: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Prepared%20SFX%20Library.7z
License: CC0. Source page includes the creators' CC0 statement.
Used: near-distance 1911 / Walther PPQ pistol, AR-15 / AK-47 rifle,
Mossberg Model 190 / Winchester Model 12 shotgun recordings.
Edits: separated individual gunshots from stereo takes, mono downmix, trimmed room tails,
anti-aliased resampling to 48 kHz, gentle transient compression and peak normalization.

2. SHOTGUN RELOAD SOUND EFFECTS
Created by zer0_sol.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/shotgun-reload-sound-effects
Archive: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/shotgunsounds.zip
License: CC0.
Used: Rack.mp3, Shell in Chamber.mp3, First Shell.mp3, Subsequent Shells.mp3.
Edits: short mechanical unlock/lock and single-shell insert slices; 48 kHz mono,
normalization and fades. Pump-action mechanisms serve as short mechanical foley
layers for the break-action shotgun, rather than playing the entire pump reload.

3. IMPACT SOUNDS 1.0
Created by Kenney (https://kenney.nl).
Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
Archive: https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/impact-sounds/87b4ddecda-1677589768/kenney_impact-sounds.zip
License: Creative Commons CC0, confirmed on source page and included License.txt.
Used: concrete footsteps; metal/plate/soft/punch/wood/plank impacts; one bell hit.
Edits: decoded to 48 kHz mono PCM, normalization and fades.
Several recordings are layered and modestly pitched at runtime for cyborg impacts,
grabs, armor failure and limb separation. They remain short event sounds.

4. SWISHES SOUND PACK
Created and physically recorded by artisticdude.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/swishes-sound-pack
Archive: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/swishes.zip
License: CC0. Creator describes recording clothes-hanger and wood swings.
Used: swish-3.wav, swish-5.wav, swish-7.wav.
Edits: 48 kHz mono, normalization and fades. Used for punch/dash air movement.

5. MEDIEVAL SOUND EFFECTS - WEAPON TEXTURES
Recorded by Ben Jaszczak and Brian Nelson / Still North Media.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-sound-effects-weapon-textures
Archives:
https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/medieval_sfx_textures_1_of_2.7z
https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/medieval_sfx_textures_2_of_2.7z
License: CC0, confirmed on source page and creators' statement.
Used: Katana Swing.wav, Katana Draw Fast.wav.
Edits: selected separate physical sword swings/draws from long takes, anti-aliased
resampling from 192 kHz to 48 kHz mono, normalization and fades.

UNITY LIBRARY SEARCH
Checked the Unity Asset Store's Free Weapon Sound Effects by SoundLab_1:
https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/audio/sound-fx/weapons/free-weapon-sound-effects-388474
It is a free asset under the Standard Unity Asset Store EULA, but the public page
does not provide an anonymous archive download. No Unity Asset Store package was
acquired or redistributed. The directly downloadable CC0 field-recording sources
above were selected to keep the project self-contained without account actions.
